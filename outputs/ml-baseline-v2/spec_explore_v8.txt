explore-v8: an object-level model toward the organizers' guide, precision > 0.7 at recall > 0.5, at the object-day unit.

Written on 2026-09-26 before any explore-v8 result was seen. Test 2026 was already opened in run-002, run-006 and run-007 (same period).

Units, labels and rows are exactly those of explore-v7 at the object-day unit, so the two are compared on identical object-days:
rows are v2 rows with a known v2 label; the object of a channel comes from the channel directory, channels outside it share one "unknown" object;
an object-day exists if the object has at least one row on that as_of with a known target label; its label is 1 if any such row has a positive
target label (horizon_labels of explore-v7); the rule is "an alarm in any of those rows in the previous 24 hours".

Change against explore-v7: instead of the maximum of channel scores, one HGB (run-002 parameters) is trained directly on object-days.
Features of an object-day, all computed from rows of that object on that as_of (all loaded rows, not only rows with a known target label),
so only the past up to as_of is used:
1. channel count; sums over channels of event_count_24h, event_count_7d, alarm_count_24h, alarm_count_7d, alarm_count_30d, episode_starts_7d,
   episode_starts_30d; maxima over channels of alarm_count_24h, alarm_count_7d, episode_starts_7d, episode_starts_30d;
   minima over channels of last_event_age_hours and last_alarm_age_hours_30d.
2. counts and shares of channels with alarm_count_24h > 0, alarm_count_7d > 0, episode_starts_30d > 0.
3. per sensor type of the directory (19 types): channel count and the sum of episode_starts_7d.
4. the 13 v2 features of the lead channel: the largest alarm_count_7d, ties broken by the larger episode_starts_30d, then the smaller
   last_alarm_age_hours_30d, then the channel id.
5. target history of the object: starts of target episodes (any left boundary) with first_alarm_at in (as_of - w, as_of] for w = 24 h, 7 d, 30 d,
   and hours since the last start at or before as_of, capped at 720, missing if none.
6. month and weekday as in v2.

Grid: the 7 targets and 3 horizons (24, 72, 168 h) of explore-v7, object-day unit only.
Training: object-days with as_of < 2025-01-01 minus the horizon. Evaluation: 2025 object-days whose label window ends by 2026-01-01.

A variant qualifies only if, on 2025:
1. the highest precision among thresholds with recall >= 0.5 is >= 0.7,
2. the base rate is <= 0.35,
3. the F1 of the model exceeds the F1 of the rule at the threshold of step 4.
4. Threshold: among 2025 thresholds with precision >= 0.7 and recall >= 0.5, the one with the largest min(precision - 0.7, recall - 0.5).
   This replaces the explore-v7 choice (highest precision at recall >= 0.5), which sat on the recall edge and lost recall on test.
Choice among qualifiers: the largest margin min(precision - 0.7, recall - 0.5) at that threshold on 2025.
For reference each variant is shown next to the explore-v7 max-of-channels model on the same object-days; that model is not a candidate.

Confirmation: the chosen variant, the same model (trained before 2025) and the 2025 threshold are applied once to test 2026 object-days whose
label window lies inside the data (as_of <= last test as_of minus (horizon - 24 h)). Precision, recall, F1, base rate and the rule are reported
whatever they are. The threshold is not tuned on test. If nothing qualifies, that is reported with the best precision at recall 0.5 per variant.
