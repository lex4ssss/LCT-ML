Explore-v13: temporal adaptation of the submitted object forecasts.

The targets remain recorded incident episode onset within 72 hours and recorded Neispraven onset within 168 hours, in object-days. No physical accident label, channel filtering, label rule, horizon, eligible row or known-gap rule changes. Features are exactly the submitted 77-feature object matrices, without numeric telemetry, domains or object identity.

This specification is committed before the new training and comparisons. The immutable explore-v12 feature cache and its input/code manifest are reused; reference predictions and metrics must reproduce run-008 and run-009. No 2026 rows or predictions enter this search. The 2026 period has already been inspected in run-002, 006, 007, 008, 009, 010 and 011; explore-v12 did not add a test inspection.

Variants per target, fixed in advance:
R: unchanged submitted HGB and its 2025 threshold, with no retraining or recency weights.
F365: one HGB trained before 2025-01-01 minus the target horizon, recency-weighted with a 365-day half-life.
W: expanding-history HGB, refitted every seven days from 2025-01-01T00:00:00 UTC, with no recency weights.
W365: the same seven-day schedule, with a 365-day recency-weight half-life.

Every training block uses only eligible rows whose as_of is strictly earlier than block_start minus the target horizon. Thus every training label window has ended before prediction begins. Each refit predicts only [block_start, block_start+7 days), clipped to the same eligible 2025 evaluation rows as explore-v8. No shuffling, validation early stopping, warm starts, pseudo-labels or use of a row's future in its own model is permitted. Training may include earlier 2025 rows only after their entire label window has ended.

Recency weights equal 2 ** (-(training_cutoff-as_of)/365 days), normalized to mean one within each training block. They do not balance classes, change labels or change evaluation weights. F365 separates the effect of recency weights from the effect of refitting. All variants use the same full historical start as R.

HGB parameters remain max_iter 300, learning_rate 0.05, max_leaf_nodes 31, min_samples_leaf 200, l2_regularization 1.0, early_stopping false, random_state 0. CPU execution uses four OpenMP threads and one BLAS thread. Feature order and object ordering remain those of explore-v8.

Class balancing, weather, numeric telemetry, domains, categorical identity, synthetic augmentation, neural networks, calibration and per-object/per-month thresholds are off. Retraining and recency-weight flags are explicitly recorded for each result. A single operating threshold per target and variant is selected on the concatenated out-of-time 2025 predictions by the existing maximum-margin rule above precision 0.7 and recall 0.5, with higher-threshold ties.

Report the same rows, AP, precision at recall 0.5, recall at precision 0.7, threshold precision/recall/F1, base rate, alarm-in-24-hours and target-history-in-7-days rules. Add known-object-only, monthly and worst-month diagnostics at the one annual operating threshold. Do not choose a model or change a threshold from a partial year.

Acceptance remains: AP gain over R at least 0.01; precision at recall 0.5 no lower than R; qualification under the unchanged explore-v8 rule; F1 at the chosen annual threshold no lower than R. Among accepted variants choose the largest AP, then margin, then lower refitting cost. If none passes, retain R and do not compute test predictions.

Before a real run verify exact training/label boundaries, one prediction per eligible row, missing-row exclusion, changing horizons, prefix invariance when future rows or future labels change, recency weights, checkpoint resume, model serialization and deterministic refitting against independent synthetic examples. After each fix execute 20 targeted smoke checks. Record source/cache hashes and implementation version; refuse resume on changed inputs or code.

Only a selected winner may be confirmed once on reused 2026, under the same predeclared seven-day schedule and label-window delay, with no test-driven reselection. This is simulated temporal deployment; arrival timestamps are unavailable, so immediate availability of observations and completed labels is an assumption. Service replacement requires feature parity, a reproducible training/decision artifact for each update and explicit applicability dates. Until then the submitted service is unchanged.
