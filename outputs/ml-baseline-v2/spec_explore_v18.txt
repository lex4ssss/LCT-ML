Explore-v18: conditional time-interval hazards for the unchanged object-onset targets.

Commit before any new onset-time/feature comparison or fitting. Preserve the submitted recorded episode-onset incident target at 72 hours and Neispraven target at 168 hours, original eligible object-days, historical training start, episode/unknown-boundary policy and all reference labels. These are recorded journal onsets, not independently verified physical accidents. No claim of achievable perfect prediction or a data-wide accuracy ceiling is made.

Derive the next eligible known onset for every source channel snapshot, using the original training-examples-v2 non-null source-label and train/validation split filter. A channel with an unknown-boundary onset anywhere in the full original target horizon remains excluded from all auxiliary intervals for that snapshot. For an object-day use the earliest known onset among its remaining eligible channels, if within the original horizon. No eligible onset within the horizon means no event in any interval; all-channel unknown exclusion means the row is absent as originally. Same-time onsets at as_of are excluded; the far horizon endpoint is included. Before fitting the derived present/union labels must exactly reproduce explore-v12 on every original train/evaluation row. Future onset times are labels only and never become input features.

Incident intervals: (0,24] and (24,72] hours. Neispraven intervals: (0,24], (24,72] and (72,168] hours. Each binary head trains on original frozen training rows with no eligible onset before or at its lower endpoint; its positive label is onset before or at its upper endpoint. Horizon-complete no-event rows remain in every head. Single-class heads use their constant class, with empty risk sets rejected. Final risk is 1 minus the product of conditional survival probabilities; this conditional factorization does not assume independent event types. Background: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6348952/ . No neural network code is used.

Feature spaces fixed in advance: B is the unchanged 77-column submitted object/target-history/calendar matrix; TN adds the committed explore-v16 106 channel-trajectory and 40 channel-relative numeric features. No new feature calculation or parameter tuning is allowed in this round. Variants per target: R; H_TIME_B; C_TIME_B; E_TIME_B equal mean of the two B risks; H_TIME_TN; C_TIME_TN; E_TIME_TN equal mean of the two TN risks. Twelve new final variants across both targets. Every score is evaluated against the original union binary label, not easier interval-specific metrics.

Each H head: HGB 300 iterations, learning_rate 0.05, max_leaf_nodes 31, min_samples_leaf 200, l2 1, early_stopping false, random_state 0. Each C head: CatBoost CPU 600 iterations, depth 6, learning_rate 0.05, l2_leaf_reg 10, Logloss, random_seed 0, thread_count 4, has_time true, allow_writing_files false, no validation early stopping. Sort each head's training rows chronologically. Four OpenMP threads and one BLAS thread. Categorical object identity is on only for C variants and their equal ensemble contribution.

Frozen training as_of is strictly before 2025-01-01 minus the full original target horizon, even for the short-interval head. Full 2025 evaluation uses the original masks; no test-2026 prediction is computed during selection. Recency weights, refitting, class balancing, object-domain/numeric/state/family/rhythm extras, weather, synthetic data, neural networks, calibration and per-object/per-channel thresholds are off. Original history and calendar remain on. Every candidate selects one annual threshold by the original max-margin rule. R uses its original 2025 threshold.

Acceptance unchanged: AP at least R + 0.01, precision at recall 0.5 and F1 at the chosen threshold no lower than R, and original qualification satisfied. Select greatest AP, then margin, then fewer features and total heads. None accepted retains R without another test look. Only a selected accepted winner may have one disclosed confirmation on reused 2026, with no test-driven reselection; service replacement requires feature/score parity.

Error audit on 2025 R: summarize TP/FP/FN/TN, next-onset interval among positives/FN, original 7/30-day target-history cohorts, and numbers of source snapshots excluded by the existing unknown-onset policy. This audit changes no labels or eligibility, establishes no theoretical ceiling and does not turn journal states into verified physical truth.

Before real fitting use independent raw-snapshot/onset oracles for exact interval endpoints, unknown precedence even after an earlier known onset, multiple channels/objects, duplicate onsets, orphans, absent events, censored/absent rows and source union identity. Verify conditional risk-set masks, probability composition, constant heads, chronological training, future-label/feature invariance, serialization and resume/input/code/feature/score hashes. After every fix run 20 targeted smoke checks. Publish aggregate results, diagnostics, base rates and both original rules; keep real rows, features and models outside git.
