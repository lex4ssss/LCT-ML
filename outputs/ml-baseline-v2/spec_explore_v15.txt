Explore-v15: histories of individual recorded alarm states.

Preserve the submitted object-day targets, 72-hour incident and 168-hour Neispraven onset, original episode and eligible-row rules, and all 2025 evaluation windows. This specification is committed before new feature-label comparisons or training. The source cache and target labels are exactly those of explore-v12. No snapshot with as_of in 2026 enters the search, and no 2026 predictions are computed.

The current reference features count alarms and target episode starts but do not separately expose battery, loss of power, disabled device and other individual alarm-state histories. The hypothesis is that these past states can distinguish precursors and different repeated regimes. This is a hypothesis about recorded onset, not a verified causal statement about equipment failure.

Use the 16 exact alarm states already listed in ml-dataset-v2/class_labels.py, without changing is_alarm or the labels. On raw pre-2026 observations at or before as_of, compute object/state daily alarm-event counts, distinct channels with the state per day and the last observation time. Preserve duplicate handling of the prepared data and orphan-channel mapping to the existing unknown object.

S: for each of the 16 states, alarm-event counts over (as_of-24h,as_of], (as_of-168h,as_of] and (as_of-720h,as_of], plus hours since the last such alarm capped at 720. No previous state observation gives NaN age. Counts zero describe absence of this recorded state, not proven sensor health. S has 64 features.
Q: additionally, for each state/window, its share of all 16 recognized alarm-state events and the sum of distinct state channels per calendar day. The latter is channel-days, not a distinct-channel count over the entire window. Zero denominator gives NaN share. Q has 96 features. Daily boundaries include records exactly at as_of and exclude records exactly at the left window boundary.

Variants fixed in advance per target:
R: unchanged submitted HGB and its 2025 threshold.
H_S: reference plus S, unchanged HGB parameters.
H_SQ: reference plus S and Q, unchanged HGB parameters.
C_SQ: reference plus S, Q and categorical object identity, fixed CatBoost parameters of explore-v12: CPU, 600 iterations, depth 6, learning_rate 0.05, l2_leaf_reg 10, random_seed 0, thread_count 4, has_time true, Logloss, allow_writing_files false, chronological training order, no validation early stopping.

Training ends strictly before 2025-01-01 minus the target horizon. Models stay frozen throughout 2025. HGB is unchanged: 300 iterations, learning_rate 0.05, 31 leaves, minimum leaf 200, l2 1, early_stopping false, random_state 0, four OpenMP CPU threads and one BLAS thread. Each candidate uses one annual operating threshold by the existing maximum-margin rule above precision 0.7 and recall 0.5.

Online refitting, recency weights, class balancing, numeric telemetry, domains, weather, synthetic data, neural networks, calibration and per-object/per-state thresholds are off. Object identity is on only for C_SQ, for future predictions on existing registered objects. All toggles and dataset/implementation hashes are recorded.

Acceptance: AP gain over R at least 0.01, precision at recall 0.5 and F1 at the chosen threshold no lower than R, unchanged qualification satisfied. Choose greatest AP, then margin, then fewer features. Report unchanged base rates, 24-hour alarm and 7-day target-history rules, known-object-only and monthly/worst-month diagnostics. None accepted means retain R and do not inspect 2026 again.

Before training verify all states, exact inclusive/right and exclusive/left windows against an independent raw-event oracle, empty state histories, missing days, missing shares, orphan channels, non-alarm records, event and channel-day count differences, age caps, future-record invariance, feature order, serialization and input/code resume guards. After each fix run 20 targeted smoke checks. Only an accepted winner can be confirmed once on reused 2026, with previous inspections disclosed, without test-driven reselection and with feature parity required before any service replacement.
