Explore-v16: observed channel-state trajectories and channel-relative numeric deviations.

Preserve the submitted recorded-onset targets: incident within 72 hours and Neispraven within 168 hours, in the original eligible object-days. Preserve labels, episode rules, unknown-boundary exclusions, horizons, historical training start and validation masks. No physical accident label is created. This specification is committed before new feature-label comparisons or training. Reuse the immutable explore-v12 reference cache and its manifest. No as_of in 2026 enters the search and no new 2026 prediction is computed during selection.

Build per-channel, per-UTC-day observations from the same pre-2026 normalized journal, using records at or before the available as_of. Each daily bin is (day_end-24h, day_end]. Do not deduplicate by event_id. The original prepared-data duplicate policy and registry mapping remain unchanged; unregistered channels belong to the existing unknown object.

T: observed daily trajectories, for all channels, aggregated only after channel-level calculation.
Use the 16 existing recognized is_alarm states with their fixed names; code 16 is observed non-alarm, code 17 is observed alarm with another value, code 18 is ambiguous or missing alarm flag. These codes are observed journal statuses, not proof of health. Determine each channel/day's first and last observed code; different codes at an identical earliest/latest timestamp make that endpoint ambiguous, with no invented event-id ordering. Also retain the number of distinct observed codes in the day.
Compare consecutive observed day-end codes only if their observation times differ by at most 720 hours and neither is ambiguous. Reversals require three consecutive eligible observations, two valid daily transitions and the first/third codes equal but different from the middle code. This measures day-end transitions, not exact intraday transition counts.
For windows 1, 7 and 30 days count: valid day-end switches, reversals, state-diverse channel-days, differing unambiguous first/last endpoints, entry into a recognized alarm, recognized alarm to observed non-alarm, switches between recognized alarms, entry into Neispraven, entry into incident states, entry into other observed alarm, and channel-days with an ambiguous endpoint. Add 7-day incoming and outgoing switch counts per recognized state (32 features). Add last-observed-state channel counts and shares for all 19 codes, using only channels with an observation strictly within the past 720 hours; missing share denominator gives NaN. Add minimum, mean and maximum last-observation age for those channels. Total T: 106 features. No silence becomes an observed non-alarm status.

N: numeric deviations for registered temperature and gas channels, calculated independently per channel, with finite decimal values and no physical-unit or alarm-threshold assumption. Numeric values include non-alarm observations. Daily numeric means and population variances are calculated from raw finite readings. Center daily means around the first available finite mean for numerically stable rolling moments; queries without sufficient history remain missing.
For recent windows 1 and 7 days, compare the recent mean with the preceding part of the 30-day window, excluding the recent window entirely from its own baseline. A baseline requires at least 10 finite readings across at least 7 observed numeric days. Standardize by the baseline population standard deviation. A constant baseline gives zero for no change and signed 20 for a change; other standardized scores are clipped to [-20,20]. Non-finite aggregates or insufficient history give NaN, not zero.
For each type/window aggregate: channels with recent numeric readings, channels with a valid baseline, channels missing a baseline, maximum/mean/90th-percentile absolute standardized deviation, maximum/minimum signed deviation, share above 3 and share below -3 among channels with valid scores. These are statistical score thresholds, not physical gas/temperature alarms or labels. Total N: 40 features.

Variants fixed in advance per target:
R: unchanged submitted HGB and its 2025 threshold.
H_T: submitted matrix plus T, unchanged HGB parameters.
H_N: submitted matrix plus N, unchanged HGB parameters.
H_TN: submitted matrix plus T and N, unchanged HGB parameters.
C_TN: submitted matrix plus T, N and categorical object identity, fixed CatBoost parameters of explore-v12.
E_TN: equal mean of H_TN and C_TN scores, no fitted combination weights.

HGB parameters: 300 iterations, learning_rate 0.05, 31 leaves, minimum leaf 200, l2 1, early_stopping false, random_state 0, four OpenMP CPU threads and one BLAS thread. CatBoost: CPU, 600 iterations, depth 6, learning_rate 0.05, l2_leaf_reg 10, random_seed 0, thread_count 4, has_time true, Logloss, allow_writing_files false, chronological training order and no validation early stopping.

All models train on eligible as_of strictly before 2025-01-01 minus their target horizon and stay frozen through 2025. Recency weights, online refitting, class balancing, weather, domains, synthetic data, neural networks, calibration and per-channel/per-object operating thresholds are off. Object identity is on only for C_TN and its equal ensemble contribution. Each variant uses one annual threshold for the unchanged union target by the existing maximum-margin rule above precision 0.7 and recall 0.5.

Acceptance: AP gain over R at least 0.01, precision at recall 0.5 and F1 at the chosen threshold no lower than R, unchanged qualification satisfied. Choose greatest AP, then margin, then fewer features/models. Report original base rates, 24-hour alarm and 7-day target-history rules, known-object-only and monthly/worst-month diagnostics. None accepted means retain R and do not inspect 2026 again.

Before training use independent raw-event oracles for daily endpoints, tie ambiguity, switches, reversals, missing days, old observations, exact window boundaries, all state codes, orphan channels, numeric baselines excluding recent readings, finite/comma parsing, constant/large-valued baselines, insufficient history and object aggregates. Verify future-record invariance, source feature/label identity, serialization, reproducibility and input/code resume guards. After each fix run 20 targeted smoke checks. Only an accepted winner can be confirmed once on reused 2026, with previous inspections disclosed and no test-driven reselection. Service replacement additionally requires feature and score parity; daily observation resolution must be explicit.
