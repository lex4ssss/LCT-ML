pumps-v2: the pump failure forecast of pumps-v1 toward precision >= 0.95 at recall >= 0.5, with a neural model and an ensemble, and a
reconstruction that also covers the two replication files.

Written on 2026-09-26 after pumps-v1 run-001 and before any pumps-v2 result was seen. Test of the primary file was already opened once in
pumps-v1 run-001 for 30 min and 1 h (0.727/0.513 and 0.778/0.559 at the 0.7/0.5 criterion). Known on validation before this spec: the
run-001 HGB reaches recall 0.385 (30 min) and 0.438 (1 h) at precision >= 0.95, so it does not meet the new target.

Data, prediction rows, labels, horizons, the split and the rule are those of pumps-v1.

Reconstruction v2. Per pump, the number of open trajectories is computed backward from the last step: open(287) = rows(287),
open(s) = open(s + 1) + fault labels at step s. Rows at a step beyond open(s) are extra rows; at most one extra row per step is allowed,
it must be the first row of the step in file order and carry no fault label, and it is treated as a normal row. Every other check of
pumps-v1 still holds. In the generator such rows are normal records written before the first trajectory, while the clock was still inside
the first 24 h. On the primary file reconstruction v2 must give the same trajectories as pumps-v1 (it has no extra rows).
Reconstruction quality is reported, not used: the mean absolute change of anomaly_score_1 between consecutive rows of a reconstructed
trajectory, next to the same value for rows matched at random within the step.

Features v2, still only the row and earlier rows of its trajectory, never step, time, trajectory length, anomaly scores, status or fault
fields:
1. the pumps-v1 features;
2. per sensor, for windows of 36 and 96 steps: change against the row that many steps earlier (or the first row) and the mean step change;
3. per sensor: acceleration = change over the last 12 steps minus change over the 12 steps before those (0 where history is shorter);
4. across sensors: mean and max of the four vibrations, mean and max of the four temperatures, and the same for their 12-step changes.

Variants, each trained per horizon on train:
A. pumps-v1: HGB on pumps-v1 features (reference, equal to run-001);
B. HGB on features v2, random_state 0, default parameters;
C. MLP on features v2: standard scaling, sklearn MLPClassifier with hidden layers (64, 32), adam, early_stopping, random_state 0;
D. ensemble: mean of the probabilities of B and C.

A variant qualifies on validation if:
1. the highest precision among thresholds with recall >= 0.5 is >= 0.95,
2. the base rate is <= 0.35,
3. its F1 at the step 4 threshold exceeds the F1 of the rule;
4. threshold: among validation thresholds with precision >= 0.95 and recall >= 0.5, the one with the largest min(precision - 0.95, recall - 0.5).
Choice per horizon: the qualifier with the largest margin at its threshold. If nothing qualifies at a horizon, test is not opened for it and
every variant is reported with its best precision at recall >= 0.5 and its recall at precision >= 0.95 on validation.

Confirmation: the chosen variant per horizon is scored once on test with its validation threshold. Replication: the same models and
thresholds are applied once to all prediction rows of the two replication files. Precision, recall, F1, base rate, the rule and recall per
fault type are reported as they are; thresholds are not tuned on test or replication data.
